# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness,
respect for differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for the
community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, harassment of any kind, publishing
others' private information without permission, other conduct which could reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers through GitHub — open an issue marked *conduct*, or use the repository's
private [security advisory](https://github.com/cagataycali/strands-minecraft/security/advisories/new)
form if the report itself is sensitive. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter.

Consequences follow the Covenant's enforcement ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban,
permanent ban.
